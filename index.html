Clipping shouldn’t feel like a scam

You’ve probably run into these. We’re building Cliptor to avoid them.

  • Pay to joinCampaigns that ask for money before you can clip anything.
  • “Not available in your country”Locked out because of where you live.
  • Payments that never arriveViews counted, work done, nothing in your account.

How it works

  1. Creator posts a campaignThey share their videos and set a budget.
  2. Clippers submit clipsCut short clips from the creator’s videos and post them.
  3. Clippers get paidPayment is based on verified views.

Who it’s for

For clippers

  • Free to join, always.
  • Campaigns you can enter from wherever you live.
  • Clear rules on how views are counted and paid.

For creators

  • Set your own budget and rules.
  • Get more short clips of your long videos.
  • Pay for verified views, not promises.

Rules we’re starting with

  • Free to join. Nobody pays to enter a campaign.
  • No income promises. What you earn depends on your clips and the campaign.
  • Only clip content you have permission to use.
  • No fake views, no bots.
  • Anyone asking you for money gets reported.
